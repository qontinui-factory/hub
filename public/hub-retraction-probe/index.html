hub-retraction-probe

A disposable product the hub publishes and retracts to prove the one-step unpublish.

hub-retraction-probe exists only to be published to build.qontinui.io and then retracted with `hub unpublish`, proving that an unpublish removes the exhibit, the launch bundle and the public record together. It stays retracted afterwards.
