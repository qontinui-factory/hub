qontinui-studio

Launch kits and labelled, provenance-carrying assets for products the Qontinui software factory ships.

qontinui-studio turns a product the factory shipped into a launch: its build record rendered as a "How this was built" page, screenshots and a walkthrough of the running product, social cards and posts, and a short film. Decorative AI imagery is optional, gated by licence and provenance checks, and always labelled as AI-generated.
